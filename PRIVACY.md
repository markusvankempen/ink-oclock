# Privacy — Ink O'Clock

**Last updated:** 2026-09-22

Ink O'Clock tells the time with a line from a book. It does not require account linking and does not ask for your name, email, or address.

## What the skill uses

- The request Amazon sends when you open the skill: the request type, the words Alexa matched, the locale, and whether the device has a screen.
- The device time zone, read from the Echo so the minute matches the clock in the room.
- Settings you choose, saved against your Amazon skill user id: paper look, quote filter, clock style, time zone choice, page-turn interval, text alignment and colors, and the line currently on the page so the screen can turn quietly.

Quotes are fetched from the public literature-clock collection for the minute you asked about. The skill does not keep a history of what you asked.

## What the skill does not do

- No advertising.
- No sale of data.
- No account linking.
- No purchases.

## Quotes

Lines come from Johannes Enevoldsen's literature-clock collection, shared under [CC BY-NC-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/).
